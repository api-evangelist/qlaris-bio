Financing led by Canaan and New Leaf Venture Partners, and includes funds managed by abrdn Inc., Correlation Ventures, and Mayo Clinic Ventures DEDHAM, Mass. — April 30, 2024 — Qlaris Bio, Inc. (“Qlaris”), a clinical-stage biotechnology company targeting unmet needs in debilitating ophthalmic diseases, today announced that it has closed a $24 million Series B financing round.
